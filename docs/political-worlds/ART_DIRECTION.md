## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Vision

### 1.1 Story Premise & Fantasy
*Metro Runner 3D* transforms Indian political theater into an exhilarating, high-velocity slapstick arcade universe. Rather than static political billboards or humorless text signposts, `VIP_EXPRESS` and `RAHULVERSE` operate like a premier animated cinematic universe—think *Wallace & Gromit* meets *Team Fortress 2*, set against hyper-stylized Indian urban metropolises, parliament rotundas, and festive rally corridors.

- **Mitron Express (`VIP_EXPRESS`):** The fantasy of the unstoppable grand inauguration and high-octane celebratory mega-rally. A golden, saffron-draped techno-traditional empire where every railway station is being inaugurated simultaneously, massive mechanical teapots pour steaming chai into ceramic kulhads, flower petal cannons blast marigolds over bulletproof motorcades, and supersonic Vande Bharat trains race on elevated tracks.
- **Rahulverse (`RAHULVERSE`):** The fantasy of the surreal, never-ending cross-country expedition and parliamentary pandemonium. An odyssey of infinite athletic stamina where finish lines flip into "3,000 KM TO GO" checkpoint arches, rogue robotic microphones flee exasperated politicians, TV studio anchors furiously interrogate empty wooden chairs, and coalition musical chairs spin frantically on hydraulic roundtables.

### 1.2 Emotional Target
The emotional target is **infectious, affectionate, family-friendly Indian political satire**. It avoids cynicism, hate, or mean-spirited caricatures, leaning instead into the universally recognized, theatrical absurdities of Indian public life:
1. **The Grand Spectacle:** Colossal flower cascades, cascading red carpets that unroll too far, endless ceremonial scissors.
2. **The Debate Circus:** Hyperactive news decibel spikes, multi-screen anchor meltdowns, flying parliamentary paper typhoons.
3. **The Relentless Campaign:** Running shoes that never stop, folding accordion route maps that stretch over the horizon, and roadside dhaba chai breaks that keep the sprint going.

## 4. Architectural Standards & Scene Composition

```
       FAR FIELD (80m - 200m)
   [ Monumental Parliament Rotundas, Cyber Skyline, Himalayan Ridges ]
                   │
                   ▼
       MID FIELD (25m - 80m)
   [ Grand Haveli Facades, Giant Teapots, Scissor Cranes, TV Studios ]
                   │
                   ▼
       NEAR FIELD (0m - 25m)
   [ Triple Railbed, Hurdle Barriers, Trains, Marigold Curbs, Player ]
```

### 4.1 Ground Continuity & Subterranean Plinths (Zero Floating Scenery Rule)
All roadside buildings, vendor stalls, rally floats, and monuments MUST employ **subterranean foundation plinths** extending down to `Y = -5.0m`.
- The top of the sidewalk sits at `Y = +0.3m`.
- Curbs feature bevelled sandstone slabs at `Y = +0.4m` to separate railway ballast from pedestrian promenades.
- No mesh may have an exposed bottom edge above `Y = -1.0m`. This guarantees zero air gaps even when Three.js vertex-shader track curvature bends the horizon downwards by 15-20 meters.

### 4.2 Track Clearance & Sightlines (The Golden Corridor)
- **Gameplay Rail Corridor:** `X = -4.5m` to `+4.5m` (covering Lanes -1, 0, +1).
- **Buffer Safety Zone:** `|X| = 4.5m` to `8.0m` reserved strictly for trackside signal posts, overhead gantry pillars, and low marigold flowerbeds.
- **Primary Scenery Line:** All heavy structures, crowd grandstands, buildings, and giant props MUST begin at `|X| >= 8.5m`.
- **Vertical Overhead Clearance:** Any overhead arch (Inauguration Ribbon, Yatra Finish Arch, News Ticker bridge) must maintain a minimum bottom clearance of `Y = 6.2m`, ensuring the player easily clears it during maximum jetpack flight (`Y = 5.4m`) and high train rooftop jumps (`Y = 4.8m`).

## 7. Priority Flagship Gags: 4-Beat Storyboards

Every flagship event MUST follow the classic animation timing structure:
$$\text{Anticipation (0.0s – 0.6s)} \longrightarrow \text{Surprise / Climax (0.6s – 1.4s)} \longrightarrow \text{Payoff (1.4s – 2.2s)} \longrightarrow \text{Reaction / Reset (2.2s – 3.0s)}$$

### 7.1 Mitron Express Flagship Gags

```
FLAGSHIP GAG 1: THE RECURSIVE SCISSORS INAUGURATION (District 2)
┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐
│ ANTICIPATION │ ──> │   SURPRISE   │ ──> │    PAYOFF    │ ──> │   REACTION   │
│ Red velvet   │     │ Giant golden │     │ Snip! Second │     │ Crane panics,│
│ ribbon drops │     │ shears dive  │     │ ribbon snaps │     │ endless ribbon│
│ across track │     │ from crane   │     │ into place   │     │ rolls out    │
└──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘
```
1. **Anticipation (t=0.0s):** A ceremonial red velvet ribbon drops smoothly across the railway tracks from twin gold stanchions at `Y = 4.0m`. Brass trumpets on flanking towers raise upward.
2. **Surprise (t=0.8s):** A colossal pair of 4-meter golden scissors mounted to a mobile crane swings down from the sky with a sharp metallic clatter, preparing to cut the ribbon before the train arrives.
3. **Payoff (t=1.4s):** *SNIP!* The ribbon is neatly sliced in two. Confetti shoots out. But before the scissors can retract, a second, even larger red ribbon instantly unfurls across the stanchions with an audible mechanical *THWACK!*
4. **Reaction (t=2.2s):** The crane operator figure comically throws both hands up in disbelief; the scissors twitch furiously, gearing up to cut the next ribbon in an infinite recursive loop.

### 8.1 Mobile Viewport Readability (390 × 844)
In vertical portrait smartphone gaming:
1. **Obstacle Silhouette Priority:** Railway tracks, moving trains, jump hurdles, and duck-under clearance signs must have at least a 4.5:1 luminance contrast against background scenery.
2. **HUD Protected Safe Zones:** Top 110px (Score, Coins, Multiplier) and Bottom 90px (Control pads / pause buttons) must remain unobstructed by high roadside geometry.
3. **Coin Path Telegraphing:** Glowing coin arcs (yellow/gold) must pop cleanly against track ballast. Background buildings must not use pure saturated `#fbbf24` behind rail jump arcs.

### 8.2 Reduced Motion & Cognitive Accessibility
- **Reduced Motion Setting:** When user enables `Reduced Motion` in game settings:
  - Deactivate camera shakes from petal cannons and gavel strikes.
  - Disable flashing news strobe lights (`flashLightMat` remains at constant dim luminance).
  - Slow mechanical rotations (scissors, flip boards, carousels) by 75% or lock them in their open/safe presentation poses.
